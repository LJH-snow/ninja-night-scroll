# itch.io 页面草稿

## 标题

忍者夜行：三分钟夺卷

## 简介

闯过五张相连地图，每关收集 3 个卷轴并抵达出口；五关共用 3 分钟和同一条生命值。近战敌人会追踪你，远程敌人会拉开距离发射弹体。灵活走位躲避攻击，再用近战反击完成逃脱。

## English Summary

Fight through five connected maps, collecting three scrolls on each before reaching the exit. All stages share one three-minute timer and health bar. Dodge the chasing and ranged enemies, then fight back with close-range attacks.

## 操作

- `WASD` / Arrow Keys: Move
- `Space`: Attack
- `Esc` / `P`: Pause or resume
- Master volume: Adjust in the pause menu
- `R`: Restart after victory or defeat
